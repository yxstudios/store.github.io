Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026

1. Information We Collect

At YX Studios, we collect the following information when you use our services:

  • Account Information: Name, nickname, @username, email address, and password (encrypted).
  • Profile Information: Avatar, banner, bio, and any other information you choose to provide.
  • Connection Information: Data from linked accounts such as Discord and Roblox (ID, username, avatar).
  • Purchase Information: Order history, purchased products, and payment details (processed through PayPal).
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, language and currency preferences.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and maintain our services.
  • Process your purchases and deliver the acquired products.
  • Personalize your experience on the platform.
  • Send you important notifications about your account or purchases.
  • Improve our products and services.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or transfer your personal information to third parties, except:

  • Service Providers: We use Supabase for data storage and authentication, and PayPal for payment processing.
  • OAuth Connections: When you link your account with Discord or Roblox, we only share the necessary authentication information.
  • Legal Obligations: If required by law, we may disclose your information.

4. Data Storage

Your data is securely stored on Supabase servers with encryption in transit and at rest. Passwords are stored using hashing and never in plain text.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data.
  • Rectify inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your data.
  • Object to the processing of your data.
  • Export your data in a readable format.

To exercise these rights, contact us through our Discord server or delete your account from the "Danger Zone" section in your profile.

6. Cookies and Similar Technologies

We use localStorage to store preferences such as language, currency, and theme. We do not use third-party tracking cookies.

7. Security

We implement technical and organizational security measures to protect your data. However, no method of Internet transmission is 100% secure.

8. Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to update this policy at any time. We will notify you of significant changes through our website.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact us through our Discord server.
